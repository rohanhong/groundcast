## Not adopted

- Guest backstory and personal anecdotes from the host: groundcast hosts are synthetic and must not have personal histories (dialogue.md).
- Ending without a recap (both episodes close with credits and thanks): T1 (S03, S05) outranks; groundcast keeps a closing recap.
- Long clips from films or TV as openers: no recorded media; and a long opener delays the roadmap.
- Listener call-outs, sponsor reads, and the "Unsung Hero" segment: not learning content.
