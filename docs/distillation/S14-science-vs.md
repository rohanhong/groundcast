|---|---|---|---|---|

## Not adopted

- Pitting two camps against each other as the frame for every session ("fans vs critics"): fits contested consumer claims, not most study materials; and it invites stating claims the passages do not hold.
- Host anecdotes and personal experiments (Collagen, the host's own injury): synthetic hosts have no personal history.
- Dense puns, profanity, and jokes at the expense of sources: tone, not checkable; risk of implied claims.
- Quoting individual social media voices as openers: no recorded clips; and unverifiable.
- Advice on what the listener should buy or do (Collagen, closing): groundcast explains the learner's materials; it gives advice only when the passages do, and attributed.
