# Brennick Valley Bike-Share Pilot: Year 1 Summary

This is a fictional summary written for the groundcast evaluation set. The
town, the scheme, and every figure below are invented.

## Scope

The pilot ran for twelve months, from April 2024 to March 2025, in two
districts of Brennick: Harbourside and Millfield. The council placed 120 bikes
in Harbourside and 80 in Millfield, at 14 and 9 docking stations respectively.
Electric bikes made up a quarter of the fleet in Harbourside; Millfield had
none.

## Use

Riders made 61,300 trips in total. Harbourside accounted for 44,100 of them
and Millfield for 17,200. The median trip lasted 14 minutes in Harbourside and
19 minutes in Millfield. Weekday trips peaked between 8 and 9 in the morning;
weekend trips peaked between 2 and 4 in the afternoon.

Use fell sharply in winter. December had the fewest trips of any month, at
roughly a third of the July figure, which was the highest.

## Riders

A survey in month ten received 812 responses, a response rate of 11 percent.
Of those who answered, 38 percent said at least one bike trip had replaced a
car trip. The summary warns that people who answer such surveys tend to be
keen riders, so this figure is likely to overstate the effect for riders as a
whole.

## Safety and maintenance

Riders reported 23 minor collisions and no serious injuries. Most maintenance
work was on brakes and tyres. The electric bikes needed repairs about twice as
often as the standard bikes, mainly because of battery faults.

## Money

The pilot cost 410,000 pounds, of which fares covered 96,000 pounds. The rest
came from a regional transport grant.

## Findings and next steps

Trips in Harbourside rose after two stations were added near the ferry
terminal in August, but the summary notes that the summer tourist season began
at the same time, so the effect of the new stations cannot be separated from
it. The council has agreed to extend the pilot for a second year in both
districts. It will decide on electric bikes for Millfield after a cost review
in autumn 2025.
