## Recall prompts

The brief asks for recall prompts: questions the listener answers in their
head, which helps them remember (retrieval practice).

- End the script with one more segment, headed "Recall", after the closing
  recap. It holds the number of questions the brief asks for.
- Open it with one connective sentence that tells the listener what to do,
  for example: "Let's check what stuck. After each question, you'll get a
  few seconds to answer in your head."
- Ask about the most important points, in the order the session taught
  them. Each question is one sentence of kind "recall": an open question
  that takes nothing for granted and can be answered in a few words from
  the session.
- Ask the listener to produce the answer: no yes-or-no and no either-or
  questions. Ask "why" or "how" only about causes or steps the session
  explained.
- Right after each question, give the answer in one or two claim sentences
  with their sources. The answer must make sense right after its question,
  for example "Two: the Pell and the Askra."
- The app adds a pause after each question; do not write one.
- In a two-host script, Host B asks the questions and Host A answers them.
